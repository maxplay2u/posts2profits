Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

This page explains what information Posts2Profits.com collects, why, and what happens to it. This site is run by Max J. Pilet.

What we collect

When you buy something or download a free resource, you give us your name, your email address and, for purchases, your payment details. When you book a session, you give us your answers to a few short questions and a link to your profile.

Who handles it

Purchases and downloads are handled by Payhip, and payments are processed by Stripe or PayPal. Session bookings are handled by Calendly. We never see or store your full card number. Each of these companies has its own privacy policy.

How we use it

We use your information to deliver what you bought, send your receipt and download links, schedule and prepare for your sessions, and occasionally email you about Posts 2 Profits. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe.

What we never do

We never sell your information. We only share it with the services above, and only so they can do their part.

Cookies

This site itself does not use tracking cookies. The checkout and booking pages run by Payhip and Calendly may use cookies to work properly.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we have about you at any time. Email max@thebrandbodyguard.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
